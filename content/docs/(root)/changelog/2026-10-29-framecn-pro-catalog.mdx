The catalog is now free captions plus framecn Pro. Every Pro component has a live preview and customizer:

- 16 animated components in [Text](/components/text) (formerly Typography), such as Typewriter Text, Kinetic Text, and Cube Text.
- 19 [Carousels](/components/carousels), including album, helix, ring, and trail variations.
- 7 [3D & Globes](/components/globes) compositions with routes, pins, and hub networks.
- 5 [Collages](/components/collages), 4 [Charts](/components/charts), and 4 [Scroll Animations](/components/scroll-animations).
- 13 [Compositions](/components/compositions), including [Node Reveal](/components/compositions/node-reveal), launch films, and feature walkthroughs.

All 15 [Captions](/components/captions) remain free under the MIT license. The free typography and transition components have been removed; their pages and `/components/transitions` redirect to the [component gallery](/components), and `/components/typography` redirects to `/components/text`.

Many Pro components switch between 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16 with a Format control, and the preview follows the frame and length you pick. Source and CLI installs (`npx shadcn@latest add @framecn/<name>`) stay part of [framecn Pro](/pricing).
