The framecn Pro catalog now lists 67 more Pro components next to [Node Reveal](/components/compositions/node-reveal), each with a live preview and customizer:

- 16 animated text components in [Typography](/components/typography), such as Typewriter Text, Kinetic Text, and Cube Text.
- 19 [Carousels](/components/carousels), including album, helix, ring, and trail variations.
- 7 [3D & Globes](/components/globes) compositions with routes, pins, and hub networks.
- 5 [Collages](/components/collages), 4 [Charts](/components/charts), and 4 [Scroll Animations](/components/scroll-animations).
- 12 more [Compositions](/components/compositions), from launch films to feature walkthroughs.

Many Pro components switch between 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16 with a Format control, and the preview follows the frame and length you pick. Source and CLI installs (`npx shadcn@latest add @framecn-pro/<name>`) stay part of [framecn Pro](/pricing).
