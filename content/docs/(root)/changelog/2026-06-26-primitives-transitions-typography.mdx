This release added four transitions and twelve typography components retained in framecn v2.

**Transitions** — 4 new components: [fade-through](/components/transitions/fade-through), [per-word-crossfade](/components/transitions/per-word-crossfade), [shared-axis-y](/components/transitions/shared-axis-y), and [shared-axis-z](/components/transitions/shared-axis-z).

**Typography** — 12 new components: [blur-out-up](/components/typography/blur-out-up), [bottom-up-letters](/components/typography/bottom-up-letters), [focus-blur-resolve](/components/typography/focus-blur-resolve), [line-by-line-slide](/components/typography/line-by-line-slide), [mask-reveal-up](/components/typography/mask-reveal-up), [micro-scale-fade](/components/typography/micro-scale-fade), [per-character-rise](/components/typography/per-character-rise), [scale-down-fade](/components/typography/scale-down-fade), [short-slide-right](/components/typography/short-slide-right), [soft-blur-in](/components/typography/soft-blur-in), [spring-scale-in](/components/typography/spring-scale-in), and [top-down-letters](/components/typography/top-down-letters).
