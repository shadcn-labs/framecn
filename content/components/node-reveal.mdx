## Usage

```tsx
import { NodeReveal } from "@/components/framecn/node-reveal";
```

```tsx
<NodeReveal lead="SHADCN" trail="LABS" src="/your-artwork.png" />
```

## Notes

<Callout title="Timing is a fraction of the duration">
  `openAt` and `landAt` are fractions of the composition length: the node
  starts growing at `openAt` (clamped to 0–0.9) and settles at `landAt`, which
  always lands at least 0.05 after `openAt` and never past 1.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Use a square-ish ratio for logos">
  `size` is the longest edge of the node. Wide ratios such as `16:9` make the
  node shorter, so pair them with a larger `gapTo` to keep the words clear of
  the artwork.
</Callout>
